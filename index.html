Questions people ask

Who will clean my home?

Ju does. She’s the owner and right now she cleans every home herself, so you always know who’s coming.

What if something isn’t right?

Tell Ju. She’ll come back and fix it.

How much does a cleaning cost?

It depends on the size of your home, its condition and how often you’d like her to come. Call or text Ju and she’ll give you a quote by phone, by text, or after a quick visit to see the house.

I already have a cleaner. Why switch?

Most of Ju’s clients had another cleaner before. They switched because the house wasn’t really being kept up. Ju cleans every home like it’s her own, and keeps you in the loop.

What areas do you serve?

All of Philadelphia, Bucks County (including Bensalem), Montgomery County and South Jersey (Cherry Hill, Moorestown, Haddonfield and nearby). Not sure about your street? Text Ju your zip code.
