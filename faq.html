Questions people ask

Don’t see yours? Call or text Ju. She’s happy to explain.

Who will clean my home?

Ju does. She’s the owner and right now she cleans every home herself, so you always know who’s coming.

What if something isn’t right?

Tell Ju. She’ll come back and fix it.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. Ju brings her own products and equipment. If there’s a product you’d like her to use in your home, just let her know.

Are you insured?

Yes, Jurema’s Cleaning Service is insured.

How much does a cleaning cost?

It depends on the size of your home, its condition and how often you’d like her to come. Call or text Ju and she’ll give you a quote by phone, by text, or after a quick visit to see the house.

I already have a cleaner. Why switch?

Most of Ju’s clients had another cleaner before. They switched because the house wasn’t really being kept up. Ju cleans every home like it’s her own, and keeps you in the loop.

What areas do you serve?

All of Philadelphia, Bucks County (including Bensalem), Montgomery County and South Jersey (Cherry Hill, Moorestown, Haddonfield and nearby). Not sure about your street? Text Ju your zip code.

Do you clean offices too?

Yes. Besides homes, Ju cleans small offices and commercial spaces. Call or text to tell her about yours.

What’s the difference between a recurring and a deep clean?

A recurring clean keeps your home in shape on a regular schedule. A deep clean is a top-to-bottom reset that adds detail work like baseboards, ceiling fans, inside the microwave and under cushions. A lot of people start with a deep clean, then switch to recurring.

Do you clean vacation rentals and Airbnbs?

Yes. Ju turns rentals over between guests: beds made, bathrooms and kitchen reset, ready for check-in.

Can you clean before I move in or after I move out?

Yes. Move-in and move-out cleans cover the whole empty house, inside cabinets and closets included.

How do I book?

Call or text Ju at (215) 758-4704. That’s it. No forms, no apps.

Ready to walk into a clean house?

Call or text Ju. Tell her a little about your home and she’ll get back to you with a quote.
